Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

NicKick is designed to help users track their progress while quitting nicotine pouches. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use NicKick.

Information You Provide

NicKick may allow you to enter information related to your nicotine pouch use and quitting progress, such as your pouch usage, nicotine strength, quit date, cravings, goals, and related progress information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into NicKick may be stored locally on your device so the app can provide its tracking and progress features.

Analytics and Tracking

NicKick does not use your quitting information for advertising purposes. If analytics or diagnostic services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and how it is used.

Sale of Personal Information

NicKick does not sell your personal information.

Health Information

NicKick is a progress and habit-tracking tool. It is not a medical service and is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

Children's Privacy

NicKick is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NicKick changes or adds new features. The date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently updated.

Contact
